# HeartTranscriptor

**HeartTranscriptor** is one of the four primary components of [Heartlib](heartlib.md). It
is the **transcription** component of the framework.

## Role
Transcriptor-style modules convert audio back into structured, symbolic representations
(notes/transcription). Within Heartlib, it complements the generation roles of
[HeartMuLa](heartmula.md) and [HeartCodec](heartcodec.md) and the text↔audio alignment of
[HeartCLAP](heartclap.md).

> [!NOTE] Like HeartCLAP, this page is intentionally light — the Heartlib Bible names the
> component but does not elaborate. The separate [MuScriptor](muscriptor.md) project is a
> far more developed transcription capability that this wiki covers in depth.
